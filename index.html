💍 A little wedding village

A wedding website where guests walk around a village as a character to see the invitation, photo gallery and mailbox. It is made for phones held upright, so on a PC it shows inside a phone frame.

Controls
Click = walk there · Drag = joystick
W A S D move · E look · Space jump
H heart · N day/night · Esc close
